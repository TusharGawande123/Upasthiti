# How to punch your attendance

One page for workers. Print it on A4 and fix it next to the attendance device.

## At the attendance device (the gate)

[Picture: the attendance device at the gate]

### If you punch with your FACE (permanent employees)
1. Tap the big button **Punch with face**.
2. Look at the camera. Keep your face inside the oval. Only one person at a time.
3. The screen shows an arrow: **Turn your head to your LEFT** (or RIGHT). Turn your head that way.
4. Look straight again. Wait 1 or 2 seconds.
5. You see **IN** (green) or **OUT** (blue) with your name. Done.
6. If the screen asks for your **employee code**, type it. You have 2 tries.

[Picture: face oval and arrow]

### If you punch with your QR CARD (temporary workers)
1. Hold your ID card in front of the camera, 10 to 30 cm away.
2. When it says **Card read**, look at the camera and tap **I'm ready**. The device takes one small photo.
3. You see **IN** (green) or **OUT** (blue) with your name. Done.

[Picture: card in front of the camera]

## Good to know
- One **IN** and one **OUT** for each shift. Punch IN when you arrive, OUT when you leave.
- Do not punch for another person. Do not show a photo of a card. The system checks every punch.
- A photo is kept only for a QR punch or a refused punch. It is deleted after 60 days.
- If the screen says **Too dark**, ask for the gate light. If it says **Come closer**, step a little closer.

## If your punch is refused
The screen shows a reason. It ends with:

**"Please visit the plant office."**

Go to the plant office. They will check and fix your attendance. Do not try again and again.

## Phone option (only if the office switched it on for you)
Most people punch at the gate. If the office switched on phone attendance for you:
1. Open the website address and add `/phone` on your phone.
2. Sign in with your **employee code** and your **password**. The first time, the phone asks you to prove who you are
   with your face or your ID card, and to make a password.
3. Permanent employees: punch with your face on the phone. Temporary workers: scan the **changing code** shown on the
   attendance device at the gate.
4. Only one phone can be signed in at a time. A new sign-in signs the old phone out.
5. Outside the plant the phone punch works only if the office allowed "outside work" for you.

Your face is compared with an encrypted face record made when you enrolled. It is not a photo. You can say no and
use the QR card instead. Questions about your data: ask the plant office.
