## What each screen is
**01 The list.** Sidebar: "Needs review" under Attendance, with an amber count badge (white on `#8f5300`, 6.2:1) that has an `aria-label` "7 to check"; Super Admin only (the other two roles never see it). One card holds
three tabs (**To check (7)**, **Rejected**, **Reviewed**), the filters, a count bar ("7 punches · newest first", live every 30 s) and the rows. Rows: left stripe amber (flagged), red (rejected), grey (reviewed), always with a word and icon too.
Columns: time (+ "Today" or the date, and the amber line "Saved offline, sent 52 min later"); employee (name + code, "code typed", or "Unknown"); plant, device and method badge; result (Flagged + IN/OUT badges, then one icon + translated text + code per flag or reason, then "Names an employee" / "Names nobody" on the Rejected tab, then the photo state as text: "Photo stored" / "No photo kept"); a 44 px "Review" button (primary on To check, outline otherwise; "Open" on Reviewed, "Details" on a repeat scan). On a phone each row is a card with the same lines and a full-width Review button.
*Filters:* date range (default last 7 days), employee code or name, plant, attendance device, code (flag or reason), method, saved offline, and on Rejected also "Names an employee" and the **"Show already-punched"** checkbox (24 px box, helper line explains it). On a phone they fold behind a "Filters (n)" button (n counts active filters). Active selects get the existing blue "active" look and a "Clear filters" link appears.
*States:* loading = five skeleton rows; error = red alert with "Try again"; empty To check = green tick tile "Nothing to check. Well done."; empty Rejected = "No rejected punches in these dates." with "Change dates"; filters match nothing = "No punches match these filters.". Rejected rows with `COOLDOWN_ACTIVE` appear only when the checkbox is ticked, greyed, with "Already counted: nothing to approve".
**02 Detail.** Right side sheet 520 px (full screen under 641 px, as the M3 punch sheet). Header: decision icon, title ("Flagged · At the edge of the fence"), time, device, plant, "2 of 7" with up / down buttons (queue walking), close. Body order: **Punch** (flag boxes: icon, title, code, plain explanation; employee; method; decision; "Counts as IN until you decide" / "Does not count"; plant; device) → **hints** (amber dashed lines, only when they apply) → **related punch** (only for travel, two devices, order) → **map** → **time** → **photo** → **review history**. Foot: actions.
*(a)* map: fence at punch time (solid blue, filled), margin ring (dashed brown), punch point (red dot) with accuracy circle (dotted ring), red distance line, scale bar, Map / Satellite toggle (44 px), attribution, then the bold line "18 m outside the drawn fence, inside the 25 m margin" and a four-item legend. Shapes differ (solid, dashed, dot, dotted ring) so it works in black and white. *map-failed:* tiles gone, amber note, fence and point still drawn. *(b)* two cards "1 Earlier punch" / "2 This punch" (time, device, plant, method, decision, View photo each), the speed box, and a map with both points numbered 1 and 2 (number plus colour). *(c)* time block: server received, punch time, delay, then a green "Time proof: anchored to server time" or an amber "⚠ Time not proven (device restarted while offline)" box. *(d)* a code-typed rejection uses the M3 "claim" box ("Code typed: EMP-0457 – face did not match ... can be a proxy attempt"); a card rejection outside the fence shows the map ("62 m outside the drawn fence, 37 m beyond the 25 m margin"); foot has **Approve**. *(e)* no employee: grey box "No employee is known for this punch." in the foot, no button. *(f)* hints. *(g)* review history, newest first, "current" tag on the top one, reasons in a grey quote; foot has **Change review**.
**03 Dialogs.** Centred dialog 540 px (full screen under 641 px with a sticky footer, buttons stacked, primary on top). **Confirm:** green icon, who box, optional "Note" (0 / 300). **Not accepted:** reason 3 to 300 (counter, required star plus "(required)" for screen readers), red warning "This punch will no longer count as IN/OUT.", red button. **Approve:** two radio cards, "Count as OUT" pre-selected with a yellow **Suggested** badge, reason, amber warning "There is a counted OUT at 08:03." (approving still allowed), button text follows the choice ("Approve as OUT"). **Change review:** shows the current review, two radio cards (Confirm / Not accepted), reason required. **Errors:** reason too short = red field, `aria-invalid`, text "Write at least 3 characters."; **REVIEW_CHANGED** = red box on top "This punch was reviewed in the meantime ... Nothing was saved." and the primary button becomes "Load the latest"; save failed = red alert, nothing changed. **Saving:** button with spinner and `aria-busy`. **After saving:** the next punch opens and a dark toast says "Confirmed. Next: 07:41 Santosh Gaikwad · 6 punches left to check".

## Colours (no new tokens)
Flagged = `--warning` on `--warning-soft` (amber, flag icon, word "Flagged"); rejected = `--danger`; confirmed = `--success`; approved = `--primary` soft; reviewed rows = grey stripe. Suggested badge = `--signal` with dark ink. All text pairs are the M1 tokens (6.2:1 or better; ink 18:1). Colour is always paired with an icon and a word, and shapes in the map differ.

## Typography
Inter as everywhere in the admin: 14 px body, 13.5 px sub lines, 12 px code text (code names, Inter with slashed zero via `.code`), 22 px bold times in the two-punch cards, titles 19 to 20 px. The admin is not under the 24 px kiosk rule. Long Marathi/Hindi strings: rows wrap, columns have min widths, buttons are `white-space: nowrap` only for one or two words (the dialog button text is the longest: "Approve as OUT").

## Motion (Motion 14 in the build; transform and opacity only)
Sheet slides in from the right 260 ms, dialog scales in 240 ms, rows fade up on the first load, skeleton shimmer. After an action the sheet content cross-fades 160 ms to the next punch and the toast slides up. The map is loaded lazily (skeleton box of the same height first, so nothing jumps). Everything is switched off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Accessibility notes
- Tabs: `role=tablist/tab`, arrow keys move, roving tabindex; the count is in the `aria-label`. The sidebar badge and tab badge say "7 to check".
- Rows: each Review button has an `aria-label` with the name and time. Table has a `caption` and `scope` headers. The count line is `aria-live="polite"`.
- Sheet and dialogs: `role=dialog`, `aria-modal`, focus goes to the title, Tab is trapped, Esc closes, focus returns to the row. A dialog opened from the sheet returns focus to the button that opened it.
- Map: the SVG/Leaflet map has a text alternative (`role=img` + label) and the same facts are in the bold caption line, so the map is never the only carrier of the distance. Map and Satellite are real buttons (`aria-pressed`); Leaflet's keyboard zoom stays on.
- Errors use `role=alert`; the reason field links its error with `aria-describedby`. Radio cards are real radio inputs (visually hidden), arrow keys work, the focus ring shows on the card.
- Touch targets 44 px (admin standard); phone dialog and sheet buttons 48 to 52 px. No hover-only information.
- Photo: "View photo" is a button that opens the M2 audited dialog; the list shows only words.

## Deviations from the spec text
1. **Spec example does not flag.** "2.4 km in 3 min = 48 km/h; limit 60" is under the limit, so it could never be an `IMPOSSIBLE_TRAVEL` flag. The mockup uses **"2.4 km in 2 min = 72 km/h; limit 60 km/h"**. Fix the example in the spec and the i18n text.
2. **Table columns:** the spec lists method, plant, device and photo separately. To avoid a horizontal scroll at 1280 with the sidebar, the table merges "plant, device, method" into one cell and puts the photo text under the result. All the data is there.
3. **Result badges:** a flagged row shows "Flagged" + IN/OUT so the worker-facing meaning (it counts) is clear; spec §4.9 uses ⚑ for the punch log, same icon (flag).
4. **Next/previous buttons** in the sheet header (up / down, "2 of 7") are an extra that supports "after an action the next row opens".
5. **"Counts" line** in the summary ("Counts as IN until you decide") is added to make rule 6 visible.
6. Tab counts: only "To check" has a number, as in the spec. Rejected and Reviewed have none.

## Open design questions (with my recommendation)
1. **Sort order of To check.** Newest first (as the other lists) or oldest first (a queue)? *Recommend:* newest first by default plus a small "Oldest first" switch; with up / down in the sheet either order works.
2. **Ctrl+K and the phone menu.** Spec adds "Needs review" and "Rejected punches" to Ctrl+K; on 360 / 768 the sidebar is the hamburger menu, so the entry (with its badge) must be in that menu (CLAUDE.md rule). *Recommend:* yes, same badge in the menu and in the topbar menu button when the count is above 0.
3. **Note shown to other roles.** The sheet is Super Admin only, but Plant Head and Corporate Head see "Not accepted" in the punch log (§4.9). Should they see why? *Recommend:* no (reason stays Super Admin only, as in §5.3 `reviews` rule).
4. **Approve warning vs suggestion.** In the spec example the suggestion would be OUT while a counted OUT already exists at 08:03. That is legal (the rule uses the state at punch time) but may surprise. *Recommend:* keep, show the warning as designed, and word it "may count this person twice".
5. **Photo in the sheet.** Only a "View photo" button (audited), no thumbnail, as in §4.8. *Recommend:* keep it; the two-punch cards also only have a button each.
6. **Real map in the build.** The mockup map is a drawing. Leaflet draws the fence version from `geofence` plus the margin ring (Turf buffer) and the accuracy circle. *Recommend:* load Leaflet lazily only when the sheet opens; show the skeleton box meanwhile; the caption line is rendered from `distance_m` and does not depend on the map.
7. **Bulk confirm.** With many `LOCATION_AT_EDGE` flags (24 for one device in the hint example), one-by-one Confirm is slow. *Recommend (extra, M effort, not drawn):* a later "Confirm all shown" for one code and one device, with one shared note. Not in the M4 scope unless the team asks.
8. **Where the digest card links.** F-48 links open this list with plant and date; the filters row already has both, so no design change is needed.
